nv-rs Windows preview
=====================

nv-rs runs Fallout: New Vegas from your own copy of the game, on the game's code translated to
Rust. This is a PREVIEW: Windows only, with bugs left. It has only been run on one PC so far, the
maintainer's, so your PC may turn up problems nobody has seen. Please tell us (see the end).

nv-rs is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Bethesda,
ZeniMax, Obsidian or Microsoft. This package has no game files in it. You need your own installed
Fallout: New Vegas, version 1.4.0.525 (the last update).


Start
-----
1. Extract the whole ZIP to a folder of its own that is NOT inside Documents, AppData, Program Files
   or the game's folder. For example C:\Games\nv-rs. It doesn't need administrator rights.
2. Double-click Play.exe.

Windows will probably warn you, because the program is new and not signed. See "If Windows or your
antivirus objects" below.

The first start finds your game (Steam, GOG, Epic, or it asks), checks its version, reads your
graphics card and sound device (about 20 seconds; a small window says what it is doing) and starts
the game. Later starts take a moment. When your graphics card, drivers, monitors, sound device,
Windows language or Windows version change, nv-rs notices and reads them again.

  Play.exe               the game
  Play with mods.cmd     the game with the mods in the mods folder (see mods\READ ME.txt)
  Settings.cmd           window or borderless, resolution, anti-aliasing, game fixes, frame limit
                         and more (next start)
  Import my saves.cmd    copies your saves from Documents\My Games\FalloutNV. Those stay as they are

Use Exit Game in the game's menu to quit. Saves reach the disk within about 5 seconds of being made,
so wait a moment after saving before you close the window with its X.


Your files
----------
nv-rs never writes to your game folder, your Documents or your AppData. Saves, settings, logs and
reports all stay in the userdata folder here. To go back to the original game, just start it: nothing
of it has changed. To remove nv-rs, delete this folder (your saves in userdata go with it).
Playing with mods links your game's files into userdata\modgame rather than copying them. A link is
another name for the same file, so if you uninstall the game while that folder is still here, the
disk space stays used until you delete the nv-rs folder too.

Your real saves are only read, and only when you run Import my saves.cmd. Saves you make here are in
userdata\game\Documents\My Games\FalloutNV\Saves. They are normal saves; nv-rs doesn't copy them back
for you.

The first start takes your settings from the game's own launcher if you have run it (it copies them,
and your real ones stay as they are). If not, it starts on the game's High preset at your desktop's
size, in a borderless window. Settings.cmd and the game's own menu change them.


Bug fixes
---------
Bug fixes are on by default. With them, the frame rate isn't held to the original's limit, the game's
time keeps pace with real time at high frame rates (the original runs fast there), and your character
lands on the floor after a jump instead of hovering a little above it. Menus run at up to 120 frames a
second (loading screens as fast as they can), and a dragged body is pulled as hard at any frame rate.

To play the original game's behaviour instead, open Settings.cmd and choose "Vanilla (the original
game)" under Game fixes. Settings.cmd can also set a frame limit (60, 120, 144 and so on) or no limit,
which is the default, and a frame limit in menus (120 unless you choose another, or none). The frame
limits only work with the fixes on. Settings.cmd keeps them in userdata\settings.ini, as Preset=fixes
or Preset=vanilla, FrameLimit=0 (no limit) or a number, and MenuFrameLimit=120, 0 or a number.


Game threads
------------
The game's threads (the main one, the AI, loading and sound) run side by side on your processor's
cores, as in the original. If the game misbehaves in a way it didn't with the previous preview (it
stops, hangs, or something acts oddly), open Settings.cmd, choose "One at a time (slower)" under Game
threads, and please tell us. Settings.cmd keeps that choice in userdata\settings.ini as the line
NV_THREADS=stepped; without the line the threads run side by side.

The game's drawing is handed to a thread of its own (the original draws on its main thread), which
makes busy places faster. If something looks wrong or the game stops in a way it didn't with the
previous preview, open Settings.cmd, choose "Main thread (slower)" under Drawing thread, and please
tell us. Settings.cmd keeps that choice in userdata\settings.ini as the line NV_RENDER_THREAD=0;
without the line the drawing has its own thread.


What works
----------
The whole game runs on its translated code: the opening, the open world, quests, combat, V.A.T.S.,
the Pip-Boy, saves and the DLCs, with the original graphics and sound through your own DirectX 9.
Regular .esp and .esm mods work.


What doesn't yet
----------------
- Mods that need xNVSE, JIP or other plugins (.dll files) don't work yet.
- Controllers don't work yet: keyboard and mouse only.
- It is slower than the original. On the maintainer's PC (a laptop with an RTX 5070 Ti, at 2560x1440
  with 8x anti-aliasing), with the bug fixes on, about 103 frames a second in Doc Mitchell's house and
  about 44 in Goodsprings. The original runs at about 120 in Doc's house. Your numbers will differ.
- Memory: the game's code still has the original's 4 GB limit, about 3.2 GB of it for the game's own
  use. A very heavy mod list can run out, as in the original. Removing this limit is planned.
- Known bugs: fitting a second weapon mod can stop the game. Sound can crackle when frames are slow.
  Some settings may not change anything. There are others we don't know about.
- A Steam, GOG or Epic overlay, achievements and the game's launcher are not used.
- Not tested yet, because they have only one PC to run on: other graphics cards (AMD, Intel, older
  NVIDIA), sound set to anything but 7.1 or stereo, GOG and Epic copies, languages other than English,
  Windows 10. GOG, Epic and some regional copies may be refused, with a message that says so.


Problems at the start
---------------------
When nv-rs can't start it says why in a window, for example: no game found; the wrong version of the
game; DirectX's d3dx9_38.dll missing (install "DirectX End-User Runtimes (June 2010)" from Microsoft);
the folder's path too long or in a place it shouldn't be. With no sound device the game starts silently.
Everything it said is in userdata\logs\launcher.log.


If Windows or your antivirus objects
------------------------------------
The programs here are not signed, and few people have run them, so:
- Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway",
  once you have decided you trust this download.
- On Windows 11 with Smart App Control turned on, unsigned programs are blocked and there is no such
  button. nv-rs can't run on such a PC without turning that off, and we don't ask you to.
- An antivirus may stop or remove Play.exe or app\FalloutNV.exe, because they are new and start other
  programs. Download only from the project's own page. Compare the ZIP's SHA256 with the one published
  with it. The source for every file is at https://github.com/slaterain/nv-rs (BUILD.txt names the exact
  version).
Don't turn off your security software for this.

app\FalloutNV.exe is nv-rs's own program, not the game's. It has that name because NVIDIA's driver fixes
and Windows' sound settings for the game go by the name.


Reporting a problem
-------------------
https://github.com/slaterain/nv-rs/issues

Press F12 in the game when you see something wrong. You hear a beep, and a numbered folder appears
in userdata\reports with a picture and the log so far. Add your words to its note.txt and attach the
folder, zipped. If the game stopped, attach the newest game-*.log from userdata\logs, and launcher.log.
Say what you did just before, and your graphics card, Windows version and game store.

Logs can show your Windows user name in folder paths: look through them first if that matters to you.
Never attach game files. Please compare with the original game when you can: if it behaves the same
there, it isn't an nv-rs bug.
